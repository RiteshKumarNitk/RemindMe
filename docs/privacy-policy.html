DoseWise ("the app", "we", "us") is a medicine‑reminder application published by [PUBLISHER]. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and your choices. By using DoseWise you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • DoseWise works fully offline. Your medicines, schedules and dose history are stored on your device.
  • You can optionally sign in with Google and turn on Family Sync. Only then is data sent to our cloud (Google Firebase) so chosen family members can view it.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or share data with third parties for marketing.
  • DoseWise is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

2. Information stored on your device only

Unless you enable Family Sync, the following never leaves your phone:

  • Medicine names, dosage, notes, food instructions and reminder schedules;
  • Dose history — whether each dose was taken, skipped, missed or snoozed, and the time;
  • Optional stock counts and refill thresholds;
  • Your display name and age, if you enter them;
  • App settings (language, sound, snooze length, theme, etc.).

This data is held in a private database in the app's storage and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Information processed when you sign in / enable Family Sync

Signing in and Family Sync are optional. If you use them, we process:

  • Account information from Google Sign‑In: your name, email address, profile photo URL and a Google account identifier — used to authenticate you and to identify household members.
  • Anonymous authentication ID — if you join a family without signing in, Firebase assigns an anonymous ID so the household can recognise your device.
  • Health‑related data you created — medicine names, schedules and dose history (taken / skipped / missed / snoozed and times) — uploaded to your household so the members you shared your code with can view it and receive missed‑dose alerts.
  • Household membership — the six‑letter family code, your role, and the time you joined.
  • Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — so a family member can be alerted when a dose is missed. It is removed when you turn off sync.

This data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Firebase Authentication and Firebase Cloud Messaging) on Google's infrastructure. Access is restricted by security rules so that only members of your household can read it.

4. Diagnostics and crash data

The app itself does not send analytics or advertising identifiers. Google Play and the underlying Firebase and Google Play Services libraries may collect standard technical information (for example app version, device model, OS version, coarse region and crash diagnostics) as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of Play's app‑diagnostics sharing in your device settings.

5. Permissions the app requests

  • Notifications — to show dose reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders / exact alarm — to fire reminders at the exact time.
  • Run at startup — to re‑schedule reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Full‑screen notifications — so a reminder can show on the lock screen.
  • Internet — only used for Google Sign‑In and Family Sync.
  • Ignore battery optimisations (optional, you grant it manually) — helps reminders fire reliably in the background.

The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files or call logs.

6. How long we keep data

  • On device: until you delete it in the app, clear app data, or uninstall.
  • In the cloud (Family Sync only): for as long as the household exists. Turning off sync removes your device from the household. Deleting a medicine deletes its cloud copy and its doses. To delete your entire cloud record, turn off sync and ask us to remove the household (see Contact).

7. Sharing

We share your data only with:

  • Family members you choose — anyone you give your six‑letter code to can view the medicines and dose history in that household.
  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — as our data processor for authentication, database and push messaging.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

8. Children

DoseWise is intended for adults managing their own or a family member's medicines. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Use the app entirely offline — do not sign in or enable Family Sync.
  • Sign out at any time from the Profile screen.
  • Turn off Family Sync at any time to stop cloud storage and remove your device from the household.
  • Edit or delete any medicine, which also removes its dose history.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your cloud data by contacting us.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, or India), you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the DPDP Act. Contact us to exercise them.

10. Security

Cloud data is protected in transit by TLS and at rest by Google Cloud, and access is limited by Firestore security rules to members of the relevant household. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by a new "Last updated" date on this page and, where appropriate, a notice in the app.
