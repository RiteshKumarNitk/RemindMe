DoseWise is a medicine‑reminder app published by [PUBLISHER]. This page explains how to have your account and the data associated with it deleted.

An account only exists if you signed in with Google or joined a family with Family Sync. If you have never signed in and never enabled Family Sync, DoseWise has no account or cloud data for you — all your information is on your device only and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Option A — delete it yourself in the app

  1. Open DoseWise and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Turn Family Sync off. This removes your device from the household and deletes your push‑notification token.
  3. Delete each medicine from the My Medicines screen. Deleting a medicine also deletes its cloud copy and all of its dose history.
  4. Tap Sign out on the Profile screen.
  5. To also remove the on‑device database, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings → Apps → DoseWise → Storage.

This clears your data from the cloud, but your Google Sign‑In (Firebase Authentication) record is removed only by the request below.

Option B — request full account deletion by email

  1. Send an email to [CONTACT EMAIL] from the email address you used to sign in to DoseWise.
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my DoseWise account”.
  3. If you used Family Sync, include your six‑letter family code if you know it, so we can identify the household.

We verify the request against the signed‑in email address and complete deletion within 30 days. We will email you to confirm when it is done.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Encrypted system backups of the cloud database may retain deleted records for up to 90 days after deletion, after which they are overwritten. These backups are not used for any other purpose.
  • Records we must keep by law (for example a minimal log that a deletion request was made and fulfilled) are retained only as long as legally required.
  • We keep no copy of your medicines, schedules or dose history after the backup window above.
