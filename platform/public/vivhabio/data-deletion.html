Data Deletion Instructions

Vivhabio is committed to user privacy. If you wish to delete your data or your account from the Vivhabio application, please follow the instructions below:

1. Local Data (Biodata Profiles)

All biodata profiles you create are stored locally on your device in an offline-first format. To delete all local biodata and images you have added:

  • Go to your device's Settings > Apps > Vivhabio
  • Select Storage
  • Tap Clear Data or Clear Storage.
  • Alternatively, you can simply uninstall the application from your device, which will permanently delete all locally stored data.

2. Google Account Data

If you signed in using Google to access premium limits or features, the app only stores your basic profile identifier to track your free limits.

If you wish to completely disconnect your Google account from the application:

  • Open the Vivhabio app and go to the Settings menu.
  • Tap Sign Out.
  • To completely revoke the app's access to your Google account, you can visit your Google Account Permissions page, find "Vivhabio", and select "Remove Access".

If you need further assistance with deleting your data, please contact the developer.
